What it does

CaptureArea, window, full screen, last area, timer, scrolling capture, magnifier with pixel colour.
Your file namesBuild names from date, time, app, window, counter and size. Subfolders like year/month.
Drop modePress a key and the image lands in your folder, with nothing in between.
EditArrows, text, shapes, counter, blur, pixelate, crop, backgrounds. Auto-redact e-mail, phone and IBAN.
Video & GIFScreen recording with sound, camera and visible clicks.
Text & historyRecognise text on device. Search your history by the text in your images.
