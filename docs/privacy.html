Privacy

CapDrop collects no data.

  • CapDrop has no network access. Nothing leaves your device.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Screenshots, videos and the history stay on your device: in the folder you choose and in the app container.
  • Text recognition runs on device (Apple Vision).
  • Microphone and camera are used only when you turn them on for a screen video.

Questions: github.com/ipstyle/capdrop/issues
